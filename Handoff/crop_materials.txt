CACAO
	Material Required or Needed:
1. Cacao Seed - Starting material for growing the cacao plant.
2. Seedling Bag / Polybag - Holds the growing medium and supports the young cacao seedling.
3. Growing Medium / Potting Soil - Provides support and a suitable environment for root development.
4. Water - Provides moisture needed for germination and seedling growth.
5. Shade Net - Protects young cacao seedlings from excessive direct sunlight and heat.


Cacao Seed Planting Process:
1. Prepare Seedling Bag - Fill the seedling bag with the prepared growing medium. 
2. Prepare Growing Medium - Use a loose, well-drained growing medium that allows the cacao seed to develop roots. 
3. Plant Cacao Seed - Make a small hole in the growing medium and place the cacao seed. Cover it lightly with the growing medium. 
4. Water the Seed - Water gently after planting to provide enough moisture for germination. Keep the medium moist but avoid excessive water. 
5. Place Under Shade - Place the planted seedling bag under a shade net to protect the newly planted seed from excessive direct sunlight and heat. 
	Sprout / Germination 
	Seedling
	Field Transplanting to Flowering 
	Tree with Flower to Tree with Fruit 
	  

	  

	  

	  

	Sprout Established: Approximately 14–21 days after planting, with a height of about 5–15 cm. The young stem and first leaves develop, marking the transition from germination to an early seedling. 
	Seedling: About 1–12 months after germination, the main stem grows to about 1–1.2 m before forming a branching point called the jorquette. During this stage, the cacao develops stronger roots, leaves, and stem growth and remains under suitable shade and regular watering. 
	Field Transplanting to Flowering: At approximately 5–12 months after germination, a healthy cacao seedling can be transplanted from the seedling bag to the prepared field when it has sufficient growth and a well-developed root system. After establishment in the field, the cacao continues vegetative growth and may begin flowering at around 18–30 months for some clones and hybrids, while many seed-grown cacao trees commonly begin flowering at about 3–5 years. The tree may reach approximately 1.5–4 m when maintained through pruning. 
	After flowering and successful pollination, the cacao flower develops into a young cacao pod. The fruit takes approximately 150–170 days (5–6 months) to mature. During this stage, the pod gradually increases in size and changes color as it approaches harvest maturity. The cacao tree may reach approximately 4.5–8 m at full mature height, although farmers commonly maintain trees at a shorter height through pruning. 
	DURIAN
	Material Required or Needed:
1. Durian Seed / Seedling - Starting material for growing the durian plant.
2. Seedling Bag / Polybag - Holds the growing medium and supports the young durian seedling.
3. Growing Medium / Potting Soil - Provides support, moisture, drainage, and a suitable environment for root development.
4. Water - Provides the moisture needed for seed germination and seedling growth.
5. Shade Net - Protects young durian seedlings from excessive direct sunlight and heat.
Durian Seed Planting Process:
1. Prepare Seedling Bag - Fill the seedling bag with a clean, loose, and well-drained growing medium.
2. Prepare Durian Seed - Use a fresh, healthy durian seed and clean off the remaining fruit pulp before planting.
3. Plant Durian Seed - Place the seed in the growing medium with the appropriate orientation and cover it lightly with soil.
4. Water the Seed - Water gently after planting to keep the growing medium consistently moist without causing waterlogging.
5. Place Under Shade - Place the planted seedling bag under a shade net to protect the young durian plant from excessive direct sunlight and heat.
	Sprout / Germination 
	Seedling
	Field Transplanting to Flowering 
	Tree with Flower to Tree with Fruit 
	  

	  

	  

	  

	Approximate time: 3–14 days after planting a fresh seed. Height: Approximately 5–20 cm during the early sprout stage. 
	Approximate time: About 2 weeks to 6–12 months after germination. Height: Approximately 20 cm–1 m, depending on variety and growing conditions. 
	After approximately 6–12 months in the nursery, a healthy durian seedling can be transferred from the seedling bag to the prepared field. After field establishment, the tree continues vegetative growth before reaching flowering age. Grafted trees may begin flowering at approximately 4–8 years after planting, while seed-grown trees may begin flowering or fruiting at approximately 7–10 years, although some may take 10–15 years or longer. In managed orchards, trees may be maintained at approximately 4–10 m through proper management and pruning. 
	Fruit development: Approximately 3–5 months from fruit set to maturity. Height: Trees in managed orchards may be maintained at approximately 5–8 m, while mature seedling or unmanaged trees can grow much taller, reaching approximately 27–40 m.

	BANANA
	Material Required or Needed:
1. Tissue-Cultured Banana Plantlet – A young banana plant produced from tissue culture and raised in a nursery. Banana is not planted from seeds; tissue-cultured plantlets or suckers are used as planting materials. 
2. Seedling Bag / Polybag – Holds the plantlet during nursery growth.
3. Growing Medium / Potting Soil – Supports the plantlet and its roots.
4. Water – Maintains moisture for establishment.
5. Shade Net – Protects the young plantlet from excessive sunlight and heat.


Mangosteen Seed Planting Process:
1. Prepare Seedling Bag – Fill the seedling bag with a suitable, loose, fertile, and well-drained growing medium.
2. Prepare Banana Plantlet – Use a healthy tissue-cultured banana plantlet with established roots and several healthy leaves.
3. Plant Banana Plantlet – Place the plantlet in the seedling bag and cover its roots with the growing medium, keeping the growing point above the surface.
4. Water the Plantlet – Water gently after planting and keep the growing medium consistently moist without waterlogging.
5. Place Under Shade – Place the planted seedling bag under a shade net to protect the young banana plant from excessive direct sunlight and heat.
6. Harden the Plantlet – Gradually expose the plantlet to normal outdoor conditions until it is strong enough for field transplanting.


	Sprout / Establishment 
	Seedling / Vegetative Growth 
	Tree with Flower 
	Tree with Fruit 
	  

	  

	  

	  

	Approximate time: 2–6 weeks after planting the tissue-cultured plantlet.
Height: Approximately 10–30 cm as the plant establishes new roots and leaves. 
	Approximate time: About 3–9 months after planting.
Height: Approximately 1–3 m as the pseudostem and leaves develop.
Transplanting: If the plantlet is initially raised in a seedling bag, it can be transferred to the field once it is well-established, commonly within several weeks to a few months. 
	Approximate time: About 9–15 months after field planting, depending on variety and growing conditions.
Height: Approximately 2–4 m when the plant reaches flowering stage.
Process: The banana produces a flower bud (banana heart), followed by the development of hands and fingers. 
	Fruit development: Approximately 3–5 months after flowering.
Harvest: Commonly about 12–18 months after initial planting for the first crop, depending on variety and conditions.
Height: Approximately 2–5 m at maturity.

	Seedling / Vegetative Growth

	PINEAAPLE

   1. Prepare the Field – Prepare loose, fertile, and well-drained soil.

	Vegetative Growth

   1. Prepare the Field – Prepare the soil and form the planting bed.
   2. Install the Rain Shelter – Set up the protective roof/frame above the planting area.

   4. Prepare the Strawberry Runner – Select a healthy runner with established roots.
   5. Plant the Runner – Place the runner through a hole in the plastic mulch and cover its roots with soil.
   6. Water the Runner – Provide adequate moisture after planting.

	TOMATO
	Tomato Materials
   1. Tomato Seed – A mature tomato seed used to grow a new tomato plant.
   2. Seedling Bag / Seedling Tray – A container used to hold the seed and growing medium during the early growth stage.
   3. Growing Medium / Potting Soil – Provides support, nutrients, moisture, and a suitable environment for seed germination and seedling growth.
   4. Water – Provides moisture needed for seed germination and early plant growth.
   5. Support Stake / Trellis (Optional) – A wooden or bamboo support used later to help keep certain tomato varieties upright as they grow and produce fruit.
Tomato Planting Process
   1. Prepare Seedling Bag – Fill the seedling bag with suitable growing medium and prepare it for planting.
   2. Prepare Tomato Seed – Select a healthy tomato seed for planting.
   3. Plant Tomato Seed – Place the seed into the growing medium and cover it lightly with soil.
   4. Water the Seed – Apply enough water to keep the growing medium moist without waterlogging it.


	Sprout / Germination 
	Seedling / Vegetative Growth 
	Flowering / Pollination 
	Fruit Development / Harvest 
	  

	  

	  

	  

	Approx. 5–10 days after planting; about 0.1–0.3 ft tall. Seed germinates and first leaves emerge. 
	About 25–45 days after planting; about 0.5–1.5 ft tall. Seedling develops several leaves and is hardened before field transplanting. 
	About 45–60 days after planting; about 1.5–3 ft tall. Yellow flowers develop and pollination occurs. 
	Tomato plants are ready to harvest based on maturity time (usually 55 to 65 days after transplanting) rather than a specific plant height.
Determinate (Bush) Varieties: Grow to 2 to 4 feet (60 to 120 cm) tall, stop growing vertically once they set fruit, and produce their crop all at once.
